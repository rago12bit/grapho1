One loop, every step in the graph

Trace it, argue it, prove the fix, check it worked.

01 · Trace

Follow the failure down, not the most recent deploy.

A change is a suspect only if every service between it and the symptom started alerting after it landed, in order. Alerts with nothing changed below them compete too: a failing cluster, a failing node.

MATCH p = (s:Service {name: $symptom})-[:CALLS*1..5]->(d:Service)
WITH d, min(length(p)) AS hops
MATCH (c:Change)-[:CHANGED]->(d)
WHERE c.at >= $from AND c.at <= $to
RETURN c.id, d.name, hops

02 · Argue

Every suspect becomes a hypothesis you can query.

Evidence for and against each one, every piece citing the alert, change, commit or past incident it rests on. The alerts are linked by cause, so "why did this fire?" is one query.

MATCH p = (a:Alert {id: 'A-9102'})-[:CAUSED_BY*1..12]->(root)
WHERE NOT (root)-[:CAUSED_BY]->()
RETURN [n IN nodes(p) | coalesce(n.id, n.name)]
// A-9102 → A-9101 → D-5201

03 · Prove

Try the fix on a copy of the graph first.

Copy the graph, clear every alert whose causal chain ends at what the fix removes, ask the judge again. The card says what the fix clears, what it leaves, and what explains the rest.

GRAPH.COPY faultline_kestrel proof_7c1e
MATCH (a:Alert)-[:CAUSED_BY*1..12]->(:Change {id: 'D-5201'})
RETURN a.id
// clears auth, mobile-bff · leaves C-5101 (INC-301), catalog-es (INC-303)

04 · Verify and remember

A person approves; the alerts decide if it worked.

The outcome is checked against the proof. Root cause, lesson, the fix's track record and a person's "not the cause" go back into the graph, and each company's weights are refitted on its own solved incidents.

MATCH (i:Incident {id: 'INC-302'}), (c:Change {id: 'D-5201'})
MERGE (i)-[:ROOT_CAUSE]->(c)
CREATE (i)-[:TAUGHT]->(:Lesson {text: $lesson})
// verification: "As predicted on the graph copy."

A company brain, not a log search

Who owns it, who was on call, what it breaks for customers.

Owners and experts

CODEOWNERS and commit history, people ranked by the fixes they approved and the changes they made.

On call, then and now

A weekly rota in the graph answers who was on call when it started, not just who is now.

Journeys and SLOs

Each journey's 30-day error budget, charged only to the journeys that need the service that broke, not every service that noticed.

Where it runs

Real Kubernetes manifests: replicas, limits, images, config keys. Two services failing on one node point at the node.

Deploy to commit

Every deploy ships its commits; the investigator names the one to read first. Real releases for Online Boutique.

Learns from you
